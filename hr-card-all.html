Contrast is WCAG relative-luminance ratio of white against the wash colour at the text area. 4.5:1 is the readability floor for body text; large display type like this survives around 3:1. Cards below 3:1 are flagged. Mode ② darkens each team colour toward black until it meets the luminance target, which is what most broadcast graphics actually do — it keeps the team identity while guaranteeing the headline reads.
